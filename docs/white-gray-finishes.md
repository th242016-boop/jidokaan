# Separate white and gray finishes — 2026-10-21

Special order now offers twelve colors in each finish family. MATTE GRAY and GRAY SUEDE sit between WHITE and BLACK, on every supported leather panel in both high and mid models. They reuse the existing protected neutral material assets; no new asset key or ordinary-order color is required.

The previous off-white base (#e6e5e1) appeared gray after shading. Gray uses a slightly darker neutral #cdcdcd with the previous finish shading. White uses #ffffff and a brighter diffuse response: broad shape/coverage shading is lifted with exponent 0.22, while native dark seam detail and the fine-stitch pass remain intact. Its broad reflected-light contribution is limited to 3 levels for matte and 1 for suede, avoiding sharp patent highlights. White suede retains short-nap variation at 55% of the prior relative strength to keep texture visible without excessive white clipping.

Native 1424×1392 alpha masks, part positions, stitch selection corridors and the final stitch-restoration pass are unchanged. The other twenty finish colors and all patterned-material mapping rules use the same rendering branches as before. Existing WHITE names remain valid in saved drafts and now render with the brighter white treatment.

These are photographic 2D preview treatments, not calibrated measurements of physical stock. Earlier notes in `bc-finish-color-samples.md` describe the initial prototype and its older palette.
